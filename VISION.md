# Vision

What Is Offside will help football viewers understand offside quickly through accurate explanations and visual match situations.

## In Scope

- Plain-English explanations of the offside law.
- Visual examples that show the ball, attacker, second-last defender, and goal line relationship.
- Small checks that reinforce the most common edge cases.

## Out of Scope

- Live match officiating tools.
- Legal advice for referees or competition administrators.
- User accounts, server-side data, or paid features.
